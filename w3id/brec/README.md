# brec

Persistent identifiers for BREC, the Brick Reconstruction Ontology: an OWL ontology (aligned to CIDOC CRM, CRMdig,
CRMinf, PROV-O and DCAT) and example individuals for documenting scan reconstructions of interlocking-brick models.

* Namespace: `https://w3id.org/brec/`
* Repository: https://github.com/Research-Squirrel-Engineers/brick-brec
* Documentation: https://research-squirrel-engineers.github.io/brick-brec/

Independent research vocabulary. Not affiliated with, authorised or endorsed by the LEGO Group. LEGO is a trademark of the LEGO Group.
