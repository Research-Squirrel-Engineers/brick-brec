Bricks (MBO)

LREC does not define bricks, colours or parts. For these it uses the Minimal Brick Ontology (MBO), an independent community vocabulary of the same group: an OWL ontology aligned to CIDOC CRM, CRMba and CRMdig, and a SKOS thesaurus of colours, part designs and identifier systems. LREC contributes what MBO does not cover: the scan, the measurement, the reconstruction and its interpretation.

  • Each lrec:BrickType is matched with skos:exactMatch to the part of the MBO thesaurus; the match is checked against data/mbo_bricks.csv, a copy of the five parts used so far (MBO commit 52a636a), including the number of studs against the label.
  • lrec:LegoModel (the physical model) is a subclass of mbo:Build, which is an mbo:Assembly (CRM E22) and a CRMba B1 Built Work.
  • The MBO namespace https://w3id.org/mbo/ is prepared, but the w3id registration is pending; until then the IRIs do not resolve.
