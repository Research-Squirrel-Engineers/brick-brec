F4Whole model: scan, reconstruction, interpretation, FDOx packages

Shown for pyramid 2. Pyramid 1 is analogous (FDOx concept DOIs 10.5281/zenodo.24305240 for the scan and 10.5281/zenodo.24305343 for the reconstruction). Noted inside the classes without a node of their own: DCAT-AP, CodeMeta, SKOS, Wikidata (closeMatch), OWL-Time (P4); every class is also a prov:Entity, prov:Activity or prov:Agent (subclass relation, not drawn). F5BREC class diagram

F6Identification: evidence, hypotheses, candidates

Confidence is a subjective estimate by the AI assistant (0 to 1), not a measurement; hypotheses overlap and do not sum to 1. Wikidata items are picked by hand (data/wikidata_mapping.ttl). Source: ontology/brec.ttl and the notes of the repository; Research Squirrel Engineers at LEIZA. PNG · SVG
