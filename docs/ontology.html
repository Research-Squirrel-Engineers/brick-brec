Data model

Classes, properties, domains and ranges are read from ontology/brec.ttl at build time: 23 classes and 16 own properties in three modules. Every class is anchored in CIDOC CRM, CRMdig or CRMinf (see CRM coverage); the crosswalk shows the parents in the other vocabularies.
