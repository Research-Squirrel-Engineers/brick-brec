CRM coverage

A class counts as anchored when a class of the CIDOC CRM family (crm, crmdig, crminf) is among its ancestors through rdfs:subClassOf, directly or through another LREC class. The CRM classes themselves are not in this ontology (it imports CIDOC CRM), so a chain ends at the first class of the family.

23 of 23 LREC classes are anchored in the CRM family.
