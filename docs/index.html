BREC: Brick Reconstruction Ontology

Release 0.4.0

From a structure-from-motion scan of a LEGO model to an idealised brick reconstruction, its documentation and its interpretation, modelled with CIDOC CRM (+ CRMdig, CRMinf), PROV-O, DCAT, DQV, OWL-Time, GeoSPARQL, SKOS and Wikidata.

722 triples, 23 BREC classes, 16 own properties. German and English labels.
